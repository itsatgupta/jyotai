- **Verified original Sanskrit passages: none.** Original text is present in the Sastri scan and Neely's digital witness, but its words were not independently transcribed and checked against page images here. Sastri OCR has obvious corruption in verse 1 and verse 9. The reviewer's job is to record an authenticated transcription and any actual variant; no OCR excerpt is accepted as authoritative.
- Translation issue 1: Sastri and Kapoor render PD 5.1 as selecting a stronger reference, but Kapoor's note reports an all-three view. Is this commentary alternative consistent with the Sanskrit, and does the primary verse require one selected reference for livelihood?
- Translation issue 2: PD 5.1 appears to use Lagna **or** Moon for the tenth occupant as wealth source, but Lagna, Moon **and** Sun for the tenth-lord navamsa route. Confirm the two distinct selectors and whether `strongest` refers to reference points or tenth lords.
- Translation issue 3: BJ 10.1 as rendered by Neely and Aiyar has the three tenth-lord references without an explicit strongest comparison. Aiyar's note infers three avocations. Do not import PD's selector into BJ or BJ's note into PD.
- Translation issue 4: BJ 10.1's relationship is `mother` in Aiyar and `father's wife` in Neely; BJ 10.2 differs over `women` versus a place/resting-place reading. Resolve the words against Sanskrit and any commentary. Compare each of seven PD 5.2–8 category lists against Sanskrit, keeping literal historical items distinct from translator examples. In BJ 10.2–3, the listed activities overlap but are not identical; confirm the limits of each witness. PD 5.9 concerns wealth ease/amount and place, not the core historical category identification, and is excluded from candidate output.
