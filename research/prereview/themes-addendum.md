## A. X10 for significations — AI PRE-REVIEW — NOT AN APPROVAL

Shared rules for every gloss below. Each one is a modern rendering of the same admitted term. None adds a new
signification, an outcome, a ranking or a causal verb. Each uses none of: brings, gives, causes, success,
promotion, guarantee, better, best, strong, weak. The Sanskrit term and its source chapter stay unchanged.

**Tradition caveat for A1, A3, A4, A5, A7 (`unverified`).** As I recall, these graha terms come from
the BPHS ch. 3 "planetary cabinet" passage: Sun and Moon as kings, Mars as leader/commander, Mercury as
prince, Jupiter and Venus as ministers, Saturn as servant, Rahu and Ketu as army. Within the cabinet,
each term is a role relative to the others. If they are modernised one by one, that parallel structure
is lost. The reviewer may prefer to keep them literal as a set, or to modernise them as a set.

### A1. राजकुमारः — Mercury
- Current: "prince or heir-apparent". Proposed: **"successor or deputy role"**.
- Reason: an heir-apparent is the designated successor who works under the head. "Deputy" is my reading of that junior-to-the-king position, and it is the least certain part.
- Confidence: low. "Deputy" interprets the term. The more literal fallback is "heir or successor role".
- Caveat: some practitioners read Mercury's "prince" as youthfulness or a learner's role, not a deputy (`unverified`).

### A2. राज्य — 10th bhava
- Current: "rulership or royal authority". Proposed: **"authority or office"**.
- Reason: राज्य = rule or dominion. "Office" keeps the sense of holding a position of rule without the feudal wording.
- Confidence: medium.
- Caveat: modern Parashari usage often glosses the 10th as karma or profession (`unverified`). "Profession" would add a sense that the admitted term does not carry, so I do not propose it.

### A3. राजा — Sun, Moon
- Current: "royal or sovereign role". Proposed: **"head or governing role"**.
- Reason: राजा = king or ruler. "Head" keeps the idea of the person at the top of an order. I avoided "top" and "chief" because they could read as ranking.
- Confidence: medium.
- Caveat: Sun and Moon share this one term, so they get the same gloss. Some traditions distinguish Moon as queen (`unverified`). That is not part of what was admitted.

### A4. सेना — Rahu, Ketu
- Current: "army or forces". Proposed: **"forces or the rank and file"**.
- Reason: सेना = an organised body of fighters. "Rank and file" keeps the idea of the organised many without the military wording.
- Confidence: low. Any non-military gloss stretches the term. "Forces" alone is the conservative option.
- Caveat: none beyond the cabinet caveat above.

### A5. सचिवः — Jupiter, Venus
- Current: "minister or counsellor". Proposed: **"adviser or counsellor"**.
- Reason: सचिव = minister, counsellor or companion. Its advisory sense is primary, and "minister" now reads as a government post.
- Confidence: medium.
- Caveat: none beyond the cabinet caveat above.

### A6. भृत्य — 3rd bhava
- Current: "servants or employees". Proposed: **"employees or staff"**.
- Reason: भृत्य = one who is maintained or paid, i.e. a dependant in service. "Staff" is the faithful modern word, and "servants" is the only archaic part of the current display.
- Confidence: high.
- Caveat: none known.

### A7. नेता — Mars
- Current: "leader or commander". Proposed: **"leader"**.
- Reason: नेता = one who leads. "Commander" adds military colour that the word itself does not need.
- Confidence: medium. The reviewer may want to keep "commander" because Mars's martial character is traditional.
- Caveat: none beyond the cabinet caveat above.

### A8. पराक्रमः — Mars; A9. विक्रम — 3rd bhava
- Current: "valour". Proposed: **"courage"**.
- Reason: both words mean prowess or valour. "Courage" is the plain modern word for the same quality and adds nothing.
- Confidence: medium. "Valour" is only mildly dated, so keeping it is defensible.
- Caveat: both terms can also mean effort or exertion in some readings (`unverified`). "Courage" does not try to cover that.

Note on प्रेष्यकः: the literal sense is closer to "servant" or "messenger". "Service" is already a softened rendering, and I propose no change.

## B. X18 compact template — AI PRE-REVIEW — NOT AN APPROVAL

**Owner's starting example (not adopted as is):** "… it brings forward your 9th (fortune, dharma) …".
"Brings" is on the project's forbidden-word guard, and an earlier rewrite was not adopted because of it.

**Trade-off.** Dropping the verb avoids any causal reading. The cost is that the sentence reads more like a label than prose.
"Linked with your …" is friendlier, but "your" plus a linking verb leans towards personal attribution. My recommended form
has no verb at all and keeps two provenance markers: "reviewed" (from X18's "reviewed significations") and
"houses it rules and occupies" (a factual relation, not an effect). The X18 clause "the themes the texts associate
with this period" is shortened to "reviewed themes". The reviewer should decide whether that loses the textual attribution.
The fixed closing sentence stays verbatim and appears once per section. If a section lists several periods, it comes after the last one.

**Recommended compact template:**
`<Lord> <Mahadasha|Antardasha|Mahadasha and Antardasha> (<span>): reviewed themes — <Lord>: <terms>; houses it rules and occupies: <house> (<terms>); <house> (<terms>). This does not predict an event or an outcome.`

**Alternative (friendlier, slightly more attributive):**
`<Lord> <period> (<span>): <Lord> — <terms>; linked with your <house> (<terms>) and <house> (<terms>) houses. This does not predict an event or an outcome.`
