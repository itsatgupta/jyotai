## X2. v.80 handling; is (d) enough; admit debilitation (v.39)?

1. **Item:** X2. Is option (d) (use only exaltation-sign, own-sign and debilitation-sign flags
   as the v.80 qualifier) a faithful minimum for v.80? Should debilitation (v.39) be admitted
   for this purpose?

2. **Proposed decision:** `cannot settle from text`.
   Narrower sub-answers I propose for the reviewer to consider (these are *not* a decision):
   - *Is (d) a faithful rendering of v.80?* My proposed answer is **no, as a rendering.** At
     most it is a partial, withhold-only convention. This rests only on the packet's English
     summary of v.80.
   - *Admit v.39 for this purpose?* `cannot settle from text`. I have not seen the v.39 text.

3. **Reasoning:**
   - The packet's summary of v.80 lists conditions that (d) cannot express: "strong",
     mūlatrikoṇa and benefic varga (for full results), and enemy sign and "weak" (for adverse
     results). The admission file now confirms that mūlatrikoṇa degree ranges, varga strength
     and planetary strength are expressly **excluded** (Admission boundary). So by the
     project's own records, (d) can cover only three of v.80's conditions and drops the rest.
   - **New point from the admission file (mine):** its boundary also excludes "strength
     inference" and "favorable/unfavorable inference". Using the own/exaltation flags as a
     v.80 qualifier *is* a strength/favourability use. So (d) needs more than admitting v.39.
     It also needs an amended or new admission scope for the two flags that are already
     admitted. The packet says (d) "also needs that small admission" (v.39), which I think
     understates what is needed.
   - On v.39, the admission file says only that it is on printed p. 32 / PDF p. 30, that it
     "gives degrees and debilitation", and that it is outside the admission. No Sanskrit or
     Hindi for v.39 is supplied anywhere I can read. I cannot judge whether it states
     debilitation signs cleanly (separable from the degree values, as v.38 was), so I cannot
     propose admitting it.
   - The v.80 Sanskrit is not in the packet either (the deferred row gives only a summary). I
     cannot check whether v.80 joins its conditions with "or", or how it treats conflicts
     (e.g. exalted but "weak"). If the reviewer adopts (d) anyway, my earlier suggestion still
     holds: let it only **withhold** (UNKNOWN) a match when the lord is debilitated. It should
     never assert "full" or "adverse" results. In every other case the qualifier stays
     UNKNOWN, not FALSE. That is a Jyotish ruling for the reviewer, not a reading of the text.

4. **Verse(s) relied on:**
   - v.80: packet summary only (review-packet.md lines 286, 590). No Sanskrit, OCR or page
     number supplied for v.80. Its PDF page is `unverified` (the nearby v.79 is printed
