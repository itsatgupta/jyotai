### P1. v.79: the Dasha of the 10th-house lord

1. **Item:** P1. v.79 clause: does the Dasha of the D1 10th lord give "wealth through royal
   patronage"?
2. **Proposed decision:** `cannot settle from text`.
3. **Reasoning:** The cited clause is the most corrupted part of the packet's OCR. "राजपते"
   is read as "राज्यपते", and "र्मपाश्रयम" is read as "र्नृपाश्रयम्". The second change
   swaps a whole letter cluster. I could not view the image. Even on the probable reading,
   there are two open questions. (i) Grammar: is नृपाश्रयम् a second result ("wealth and
   royal patronage"), or does it describe the wealth ("wealth through patronage")? (ii) The
   packet's domain note puts P1 under "status/patronage (rājya)". In राज्यपतेः, however,
   *rājya* names the **house** (lord of the 10th). It is not the result. The result is
   वित्तं (+ नृपाश्रयम्). This bears on X6 and X8. Also, the packet's draft user wording
   ("bringing **gain** through patronage") renders *vitta* (wealth) with the word the packet
   uses for *lābha* in P2. That blurs P1 and P2. My suggestion: if the reading is confirmed,
   use "wealth or material support" for *vitta* and keep "gain" for P2.
4. **Verse(s) relied on:** BPHS अथ दशाफलाध्यायः, v.79, printed p. 438 / PDF p. 436 (packet
   locator). OCR (raw, as in the packet): `-वित्तं राजपतेर्मपाश्रयमथोलाभं हिलाभेशितु`.
   Probable reading (packet, unverified): `वित्तं राज्यपतेर्नृपाश्रयम्`.
5. **Tradition / commentator differences:** Other BPHS editions and translations are generally
   understood to number the Dasha-phala chapter and its verses differently from this edition

6. **Confidence:** low.
7. **What the reviewer must check:** PDF p. 436 image: (a) राजपते or राज्यपते; (b) the cluster
